# Baper Rasta — Feature Catalogue (researched 1 Nov 2026, 2 play sessions + Guide + Disclaimer)
## For building an INSPIRED original game — feature types only, no code/art/text copied.

Verified in direct play:
- Live low-poly 3D street behind the home menu; title screen with start button, start-location picker (5 Dhaka areas + random), How to Play panel, Most Wanted leaderboard button, Guide + Disclaimer links, maker credit, social buttons, subscribe (email) dialog.
- Wide divided carriageway: median with bushes, dashed lanes, sidewalks with coloured kerbs, buildings, shops with awnings, trees, poles + overhead wires, metro pillars/deck overhead, billboards/posters, stalls, pedestrians (scatter when approached).
- Player: colourful battery auto-rickshaw with driver figure, chase camera behind/above. Opposite traffic runs the other side of the median.
- Traffic seen: cars/SUV, CNG, green + yellow bus, pedal rickshaw, other battery autos, pedestrians on sidewalks/crossings.
- HUD: National Loss (top-left, big), Charge % + bar (top-right), circular minimap with roads/player/points, nearest-charging-point arrow pill with distance, round pause + sound buttons, area-name banner when entering a place, VIP event banner/toast, scrolling satirical news ticker at the bottom, resume countdown.
- No speedometer / health bar / missions — endless run on a Dhaka map, time of day varies per run (morning/afternoon/dusk/night).
- Controls (listed): W/Up drive, S/Down brake, A/D steer, Space/Shift boost (uses more charge, works alone), H horn (nudges rickshaw/auto/bike/CNG aside), B sound bomb (nudges small cars aside), M sound on/off, P/Esc pause, Enter start, R restart on newspaper, Esc back to menu. Mobile: thumb arrows + horn/bomb left, big drive button right, boost/brake (layout differs portrait/landscape), slide-between-buttons allowed.
- Charge starts 100%, drains with driving/boost; sound toggle works. Most Wanted: poster-style top-20 leaderboard, This week / All time tabs.

From the game's own Guide (not reached in direct play — browser play could not hold W continuously, so no crash/game-over was triggered):
- Scoring: every broken vehicle/shop raises National Loss; consecutive crashes build combo; same vehicle pays once; harder hits pay more; side-mirror graze pays a small amount; VIP car and police car have fixed hit values; wrong-way driving pays 1.5x; near-miss of a bus/truck also pays; hawker-zone shop hits are capped/half-counted.
- Bus/truck (red light ring underneath them) and the pink women's bus end the run on contact. Open manhole/ditch also ends the run after an early grace period.
- Crowd chase: after crashes people chase and grab the auto; enough grabbers (or stopping while grabbed) ends the run; boosting/sharp turns shake them off. Hawker zones make crowds angrier.
- Police: hitting a police car starts a siren chase. Bribe/tea-money stop (pink mark): paying a rising % of your National Loss (min amount) closes the case and ends the chase; too small a loss = refused.
- Charging: stop on a yellow mark by a power pole to recharge; minimap arrow points to the nearest one; low charge = auto runs at under half speed.
- Obstacles/extras: bikes, trucks, tea stalls, protest cart (acts as a ramp, flight pays), protest banner (tearing pays), open manholes marked with stick + red cloth, dug-up signs, potholes (one has fishing), muddy broken stretches (~100 m), hawker stalls on sidewalks/road edges.
- Game over: fictional-newspaper front page with headline, crash-location photo, total loss, a "what this money could have bought" comparison; shareable as image/video; shared link carries loss + location and challenges a friend; name entry feeds the Most Wanted poster.
- Guide/FAQ topics: free, no download/account, works on Android/iPhone/PC browsers, graphics scale down on cheap phones, installable from browser (no APK), satire not real-life instruction. Disclaimer topics: fictional satire, no political affiliation, don't imitate stunts, mob scenes are satire not endorsement (justice through law), respect for auto drivers / road safety for all, health warning (flashing lights, loud sounds, screen shake).
- Landmarks/areas (guide-reported): real Dhaka geography, metro stations Motijheel→Uttara, Shaheed Minar, Curzon Hall, Parliament, Shapla Chattar, Hatirjheel, Karwan Bazar, etc.
